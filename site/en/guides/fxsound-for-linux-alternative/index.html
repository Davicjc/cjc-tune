CJC Tune / Guides

An FxSound alternative for Linux

FxSound is Windows only. Here is how to get the same effects on Linux.

FxSound is a simple Windows app that makes sound fuller without technical knowledge. It has no official Linux version.

CJC Tune grew out of FXSound for Linux, a community open-source project, and has its own interface, code layout and features. It applies a 10-band equalizer and five effects to the whole system: Fidelity, Ambience, Dynamic boost, 3D surround and HyperBass.

Coming from FXSound for Linux?

Quit it before opening CJC Tune. On its first launch CJC Tune brings over your EQ, effects and output, and it imports files exported by it.

CJC Tune is not affiliated with FxSound for Windows. Effect names are used only so you can recognise what each one does.

CJC Tune 1.0.0 Free and open source. AppImage and .deb for any Linux with PipeWire or PulseAudio.
